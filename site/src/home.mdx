## What it does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Run pipelines" icon="rocket">
    Typed YAML, every step a container. Independent steps run in parallel; unchanged steps come from a cache. Builds
    push images to ECR only for trusted pushes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Design what should exist" icon="puzzle">
    Draw a blueprint on a canvas, or add reviewed templates (Lambda, Step Functions, data pipelines, EKS). Forgeline
    writes the OpenTofu, previews and checks the plan, and builds exactly that plan once a different administrator approves.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Teams of agents" icon="star">
    Agents with instructions, skills, tools and their own model. Give a team a goal: the lead splits the work, the
    members look into it at the same time, and the lead writes one answer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Workflows on a canvas" icon="seti:pipeline">
    Draw agent workflows like n8n: agents, lookups, decisions, questions for people, merges and loops, started by hand,
    on a schedule, by a webhook or by a failed run.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Any model" icon="setting">
    Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, or a local model through Ollama. Prices, monthly limits, a
    fallback, and a live log of every call.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Missions" icon="approve-check">
    Give an agent a goal. It reads, proposes fixes, waits for your OK, checks its work and reports. Every finding names
    the lookup that showed it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Watch your cloud" icon="magnifier">
    A map of your AWS account, a page per service, Kubernetes, Google Cloud and Azure, and what needs a look.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Guardrails and controls" icon="warning">
    Policy packs (Trivy, Checkov, Conftest) on every preview, single sign-on, approval rules, per-service switches for
    the AI, and an audit log you can export.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## How it stays safe

- **Nothing changes your cloud without a person.** Every change is a proposal a different administrator approves, and
  exactly what was previewed is what runs.
- **The AI can never do more than the person who asked.** Agents work with the asker's role, and you choose per AWS
  service whether the AI may see it, only look, or propose changes.
- **Claims must be backed.** A mission's findings must name a lookup the agent really ran; the rest are left out.
- **Everything is audited**, including refusals, and the log can be exported.

Why it works the way it does is written down in the [design notes](/forgeline/design/001-execution-engine/).
